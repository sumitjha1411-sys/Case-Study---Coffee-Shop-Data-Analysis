The Coffee Shop Data dataset consists of several tables, each focusing on different aspects
of the business:

-- Orders: Records of customer orders, including order IDs, timestamps, item IDs,
quantities, customer names, and whether the order was for dine-in or takeout.

-- Items: Details of menu items, including item IDs, SKUs, names, categories, sizes, and
prices. 

-- Recipes: Information on how each menu item is made, listing required ingredients
and quantities. 

-- Ingredients: A list of ingredients used in the coffee shop, including their IDs, names,
weights, measurements, and prices. 

-- Inventory: Current stock levels of each ingredient. 

-- Staff: Information on coffee shop staff, including their IDs, names, positions, and
salary rates. 

-- Shift: Details of work shifts, including shift IDs, days, start times, and end times. 
-- Rota: Staff work schedules, linking staff members to specific shifts.
